# Technical references

1. Norman P. Jouppi. Improving Direct-Mapped Cache Performance by the Addition of a Small Fully-Associative Cache and Prefetch Buffers. ISCA 1990. [Primary paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs/academic/class/15740-f15/www/papers/jouppi90.pdf).
2. Tien-Fu Chen and Jean-Loup Baer. Effective Hardware-Based Data Prefetching for High-Performance Processors. IEEE Transactions on Computers, 1995. [Author-hosted paper](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~baer/chenieee.pdf).
3. Santhosh Srinath, Onur Mutlu, Hyesoon Kim and Yale N. Patt. Feedback Directed Prefetching Improving the Performance and Bandwidth-Efficiency of Hardware Prefetchers. HPCA 2007. [Author-hosted paper](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~omutlu/pub/srinath_hpca07.pdf).
4. Arjun Seshadri, Alex Peysakhovich and Johan Ugander. Discovering Context Effects from Raw Choice Data. ICML 2019, PMLR 97, pages 5660-5669. [Primary proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/seshadri19a.html).
5. Nitesh Kumar Shah. Learning from Comparison Data. Supplied first draft, 2026, sections 4.4-4.6. The private source PDF is not redistributed. The report reproduces illustrative arithmetic and analytical parameter storage, not its large training-time experiment.
6. [Nitesh Level 1 source commit](https://github.com/rbhdsks/Micorn-day/commit/e0ab37c30e7a02dd1f3d868aef956ee88104a8b0). Imported analysis and modelled matrix traces.
